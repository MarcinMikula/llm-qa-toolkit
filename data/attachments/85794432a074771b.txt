Great question — these are two completely different types of insurance:

**OC (Odpowiedzialnosc Cywilna) — Third Party Liability:**
- **Mandatory** by law for all vehicle owners in Poland
- Covers damage you cause to other people or their property
- Regulated by the Motor Insurance Act (ustawa o ubezpieczeniach obowiazkowych)
- Minimum coverage amounts set by law
- Does NOT cover damage to your own vehicle

**AC (Auto Casco) — Comprehensive/Own Damage:**
- **Voluntary** — your choice whether to take it out
- Covers damage to your own vehicle (accident, theft, vandalism, weather)
- Regulated by the insurer's OWU (General Terms and Conditions)
- Coverage scope, exclusions, and deductibles vary by insurer and policy
- More expensive but protects your own asset

In short: OC protects others from you, AC protects your own car. Would you like to know more about either type?
