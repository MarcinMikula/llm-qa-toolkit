Storm damage to your roof may be covered under your home insurance, but whether it applies depends on several factors:

**Coverage scope**: Most home insurance policies (ubezpieczenie mieszkania/domu) include storm and wind damage, but you need to check your policy document.

**Key things to verify:**
- Is storm damage explicitly listed in your coverage? (zakres ubezpieczenia)
- Are there any exclusions that might apply? (wyłączenia odpowiedzialności)
- Does a deductible or franchise apply? (franszyza redukcyjna/integralna)
- Was the damage caused by a named peril (e.g. wind speed threshold)?

**Next steps:**
1. Document the damage with photos immediately
2. Check your policy document for storm coverage terms
3. File a claim as soon as possible — delays may affect your case
