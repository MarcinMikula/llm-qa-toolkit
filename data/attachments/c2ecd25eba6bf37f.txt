To cancel your subscription, here's what you need to know:

- **Check your contract** — most plans have a minimum notice period (usually 30 days)
- **Cancellation channels**: online account portal, customer service hotline, or in-store
- **Early termination** — if you're within a contract period, early termination fees may apply
- **Keep confirmation** — always get written confirmation of your cancellation request
