Our loyalty programme rewards long-term customers with discounts on their energy bills. Here's how it works and what you need to do:

**Conditions to qualify:**
- Active contract for at least 12 months
- Account in good standing (no overdue payments)
- Registered for online account management

**Steps to get your discount:**
1. Log in to your online account at our website
2. Navigate to 'Loyalty Programme' in your profile
3. Click 'Activate discount' — this must be done manually
4. Discount will be applied to your next billing cycle

The discount is not applied automatically — you must activate it. Would you like help logging in to your account?
