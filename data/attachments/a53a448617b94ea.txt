Here's what to do after a car accident to ensure your claim is processed smoothly:

1. **Ensure safety first** — move to a safe location if possible
2. **Call police** if there are injuries or significant damage
3. **Document everything** — take photos of all vehicles, damage, and the scene
4. **Exchange details** with other parties: name, insurance, registration
5. **Report within 24 hours** — notify us as soon as possible after the accident
6. **Gather witness information** if available
7. **Complete the claim form** — available in our app or online portal
