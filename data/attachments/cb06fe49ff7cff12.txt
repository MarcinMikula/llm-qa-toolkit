If you suspect your card has been cloned, act immediately:

1. **Block your card now** — use our banking app, website, or call the 24/7 hotline
2. **Review recent transactions** — identify any unauthorised charges
3. **Report to the bank** — file a formal fraud report so we can investigate
4. **File a police report** — especially if significant amounts were taken
5. **Request a new card** — we'll issue a replacement with a new number

Time is critical with card fraud — the sooner you block the card, the less exposure you have. Our fraud hotline is available 24/7.
